# Performance Direction

## Prompt-Only Ownership

For requested acting direction, the caller composes a model-independent plan,
any requested coverage and the model-specific cue block. Leaves consume supplied
facts and never invoke each other. Ordinary prompt writing does not require a
whole-scene workflow or an automatic review.

Return plans and cues in chat. Save a draft under the requested project only on
explicit request. Existing source locators, beat IDs and hashes may be carried
forward; do not invent files, hashes, IDs, production stages or approval records.
An isolated prompt can supply intention, target, trigger and locks inline.

## Creative Premise and Handoff

For new, underspecified moving-image work, settle the intended audience response,
what carries attention, and why the ending matters before treating a proposal
as an executable direction. Read these from supplied intent where possible.
Ask only for material gaps; offer genuinely different premises only while the
idea is unresolved, not for a settled single-prompt edit. An observational
ending may sustain attention or leave a question open; no reversal is required.

Return a compact chat handoff alongside the prompt, proportionate to the request:

- Premise and locks, distinguishing supplied facts from proposed interpretations.
- Picture and edit intent: what to reveal, withhold, emphasize or sustain, and
  which listening or recovery beat a trim must retain.
- Early sound intent: the role of speech, ambience, an accent, or silence in
  that attention and ending. Preserve the selected audio route; no final audio
  asset or separate-audio workflow is required to discuss intent.
- Observable acceptance suggestions tied to the brief, such as a listener
  continuing a task through a refusal, a hurried exchange remaining intelligible,
  or a sound withheld until the reveal. These are proposed checks, not scores
  or approvals. Final cue timing follows the measured edit.

No mandatory document, production registry, automatic review or new stage is
introduced. Save only on explicit request. When media is available and inspection
is authorized, record actual playback/listening evidence for these suggestions;
otherwise leave the outcome unverified.

## Performance Handoff

Preserve exact dialogue, language, register, medium, framing, timing and outcome.
Separate established facts from proposed interpretations and missing context.
For scene work, identify the event, each principal's goal and tactic, ongoing
physical channel, listening or deliberate non-response, and a compact ledger:

**Trigger -> registration -> attempt maintained or changed -> consequence.**

Use `acting-direction` for the plan and `seedance-acting-console` for concise
Seedance cues. The selected model remains unchanged. `scene-craft` applies its
five-element engine only when that framework is selected. Neither an audience
value shift nor a mood label is a compulsory character emotion.

If shot planning is requested, pass the relevant beats to `seedance-shot-design`.
Reserve time for listening and response; use cues the crop, camera side and
subject scale can reveal. Holds, silence, ambiguity and repeated stillness are
valid. Do not add cuts, gestures or a reversal to satisfy a quota.

Body speed, amplitude, interruptions and recovery follow the approved pursuit
and physical action. Actor energy, camera energy and edit rhythm are separate
choices. Energetic dialogue is valid when speaker, words and relevant responses
remain readable at the planned framing and speed; speech is not an energy cap.
Quiet acting and sustained stillness are equally valid. A single burst with
holds or slower movement is an optional remedy for a demonstrated failure,
not an acceptance rule. Do not erase repeated action or add recovery holds
across locked timing. Repeated framing, equal durations and multiple holds
need no exception count; no fixed cadence, coverage, turn or "wow" quota applies.

For requested fictional staged fights, `seedance-fight-choreography` supplies
participant and prop states, apparent contact or avoidance, reaction and settled
end state. Preserve the selected style or leave it unspecified; no default
fighting school, new winner, injury or real-world combat instruction.

Static sheets, empty plates and sound beds do not need acting plans.
Preservation-only edits inherit performance and check its preservation instead
of inventing new acting. A masked face or fixed camera alone is not an exemption.

## Optional Manual Review

Only a user-invoked `/prompt-review` applies these semantic checks:

- `acting.plan_bound`: trace critical supplied intent, triggers, listener tasks
  and consequences to readable, time-compatible cues. Missing material evidence
  is incomplete; omitted critical beats or contradicted locks are MAJOR.
- `fight.readable_exchange`: for authored fight action, trace stable identities,
  start/end geometry, apparent interaction, reactions, timing and prop ownership.
  Hidden contact is not demonstrated contact.

Record `not_applicable` with a concrete reason for non-performance assets,
non-fight work or preservation-only edits. Changed source intent or cue content
makes an earlier assessment stale; never invent passing evidence. These are
manual review criteria, not automatic classifiers or generation gates.
Do not load review while composing or require it before handing off a prompt.

## Evidence Limits

Prompt-only work predicts a readable performance; it does not establish a
successful take. When generated output is actually supplied or separately
authorized, inspect continuous playback and listen where sound matters.
Unseen motion and unheard delivery remain unresolved. Recheck the actual edit
so trimming does not remove an accepted response. A transcript, sparse frames,
provider success or synthetic test does not prove acting quality.

Native Seedance 2.5 draft settings and separate generation authorization remain
governed by the existing production and transport contracts.
